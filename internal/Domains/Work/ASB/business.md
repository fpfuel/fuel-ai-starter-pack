## How an order moves

1. Customer asks for a quote.
2. She sources the item (supplier site, SAGE, hawaiipromo.com) and gets the details: quantity,
   delivery date, decoration (confirm)
3. She builds the quote spreadsheet and sends it (5 to 35 minutes today) (confirm)
4. Customer says yes. She does the artwork (confirm)
5. She fills out the order form and the supplier purchase order, sends the PO to the supplier
   and both documents to the order-entry assistant (confirm)
6. The order-entry assistant keys the order into billing (confirm)
7. The order-entry assistant routes the proof and later the tracking to the customer (confirm)
8. The invoicing assistant handles any invoice issue (confirm)
