# Working with me

- Voice first. I dictate with Wispr Flow: expect run-on sentences, lists out of order and filler
  words. Pull out the meaning and confirm it back in one line.
- One question at a time. Ask, wait, then ask the next. Never post a list of questions.
- Plain English and short replies. No jargon and no code talk unless I ask.
- I work with AI on my own; my team does not use it. Write what I will hand them, never
  something that assumes they will read Claude's output.
- When you write or change a file, tell me its full path.
- When I say "add this to my list", it goes in today's focus file
  (`internal/Domains/Work/ASB/daily/`).
