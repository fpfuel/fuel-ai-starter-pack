# Copilot prompts for Karen

Paste one into Copilot in Outlook. Fill the square brackets first. Paste the answer back
into Claude and say what you want done with it.

These are shaped for a 200-plus email backlog where quotes, orders and noise are mixed
together. Copilot's results vary by tenant; note what works under "Results" at the bottom.

## 1. Inbox triage

Look at my unread email from the last 7 days. Sort it into five groups: quote requests,
order updates (proofs, tracking, backorders), customer questions waiting on my reply,
supplier emails, and everything else. For the first three groups list sender, company,
one line on what they need, and the date. Skip newsletters and marketing.

## 2. Replies I owe

Which emails from the last 14 days asked me a question or for something and have no reply
from me? List sender, subject, what they asked, and how many days it has been waiting.
Oldest first.

## 3. Open loops with one customer

Find every email with [customer or person] in the last 60 days. Summarize in three parts:
what they asked for, what I promised, and what is still open. Include quantities, dates
and dollar figures that appear.

## 4. Summarize this thread

Summarize this thread: what was decided, what is still open, who owes the next step, and
any numbers, quantities, or dates mentioned. Five lines or fewer.

## 5. Draft a reply

Draft a short reply to this email that says [what you want to say]. Friendly and plain,
under 120 words, sign off as Karen.

## 6. Quote requests only

Find emails from the last 30 days where a customer asks for pricing or a quote. For each
give the customer, the item, the quantity, the event or in-hands date, the ship-to
location, and whether I have already replied.

## 7. Orders in flight

Find emails from the last 30 days about orders already placed: proofs, tracking,
backorders, delivery problems. Which ones need something from me rather than from
my assistants?

## 8. Noise report

Which senders in my inbox over the last 30 days are marketing, newsletters or automatic
notifications? List sender and count so I can set rules.

## 9. Today's five

Looking at my calendar for today and my unread email, what are the five most
time-sensitive things? One line each, with the deadline if there is one.

## 10. Meeting prep

I am meeting [customer] on [date]. Summarize everything we have emailed about in the last
6 months and anything still unresolved. Short bullets.

## 11. Find the order

Find emails about [customer or order number] from the last 90 days so I can match them
against the status in M-Files. List date, sender, subject and one line of what it says.

## 12. End of day

What came in today from customers or suppliers that I have not opened yet? Skip
newsletters. Sender, subject, one line each.

## 13. Describe an M-Files list for Claude

Copilot cannot see M-Files. Open your orders and quotes list in M-Files, sort by due
date, and dictate it to Claude in this shape: customer, item, status, due date. Claude
turns it into the focus list.

## 14. Meeting recap

Using the recap and transcript of my meeting [meeting name] on [date], list: who was there,
what was decided, what I promised and by when, what they promised and by when, and any
quote, order or artwork request with item, quantity and date. Short bullets.
