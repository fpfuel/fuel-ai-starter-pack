| `Context/calendar.md` | Dated milestones | When anything has a date |
| `Context/obligations.md` | Promises and deadlines with an owner | When planning a day or week |
| `Me.md` | Her own lessons and rules of thumb | Before advising on a decision |
| `Decisions/log.md` | What was decided and why | When something seems settled, or two sources disagree |
| `memory/` | One learned fact per file; index `MEMORY.md` | When a task touches something learned before |
| `LLM Wikis/` | Reference collections she builds over time | When a question calls for them |
| `Templates/` | Templates for a folder map, a memory and a skill | When making any of those |
